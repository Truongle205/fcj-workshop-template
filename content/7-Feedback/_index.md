### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
FCAJ provides a professional, friendly, and open working environment. Members are always willing to support, share knowledge, and create favorable conditions for interns to adapt, learn, and grow throughout the program.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentor provides dedicated guidance, shares practical experiences, and encourages me to actively research and solve problems independently. The admin team also provides necessary documents, information, and support throughout my participation in the program.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The assigned tasks are closely related to my academic background while also allowing me to explore new technologies and better understand how to apply theoretical knowledge to real-world situations.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
The program helps me improve technical skills, teamwork, task management, and communication skills in a professional environment. The experiences and advice shared by mentors also provide me with better direction for my future career development.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
FCAJ has a strong culture of support and collaboration. Members are always willing to discuss, share ideas, and help each other overcome challenges. This experience helped me better understand the importance of teamwork in a technology environment.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The program provides good conditions for interns to learn and develop through training sessions, workshops, and opportunities to gain practical knowledge and experience.

### Additional Questions

- The thing that impressed me the most was the opportunity to learn from mentors and experience the actual working process in the technology industry.

- The program could be improved by organizing more specialized knowledge-sharing sessions and practical project activities to help participants gain deeper experience.

- I would recommend FCAJ to my friends because it is an environment that helps students develop knowledge, skills, and better career orientation.

### Suggestions & Expectations

- Continue maintaining mentoring activities, workshops, and experience-sharing sessions for future interns.
- Provide more practical projects so participants have more opportunities to apply their knowledge.
- I hope to have the opportunity to continue contributing and participating in FCAJ technology programs in the future.
